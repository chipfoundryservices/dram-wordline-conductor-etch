# Glossary: DRAM Word-Line Conductor Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**AA (active area):** Silicon island that holds the access transistors of two DRAM cells. Reference: 14 nm wide, tilted 20° from the bit-line direction (Book #26). (Ch. 1.2)

**Actinometry:** Ratio of an emission line to a nearby Ar line, used to track relative species density (e.g., F 703.7 / Ar 750.4). (Ch. 15.1, App. B.5)

**ALE (atomic-layer etching):** Recess in self-limiting half-cycles (modify, remove). EPC ≈ 0.25 nm for W in Cl/Ar⁺. (Ch. 6.5)

**Antenna ratio:** Exposed conductor area divided by the gate-oxide area under it. About 0.05 for a buried word line, so classic antenna damage is weak. (Ch. 3.6.1)

**APC (advanced process control):** Feed-forward and feedback adjustment of the main-step time from post-CMP height, word-line CD, and post-etch metrology. (Ch. 15.5)

**ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching):** Fall of the recess rate as the slot above the metal deepens. ER/ER₀ = 1/(1 + kA). (Ch. 3.2–3.3)

**ARDE coefficient (k):** Parameter of the linear ARDE form. Reference 0.035 (pulsed); 0.055 (CW). k ≈ β/2 with the simple slot transmission. (Ch. 3.2.2, 6.3)

**Array edge:** Boundary of the cell array where multiple-patterned word lines end and CMP erosion changes. Protected by dummy word lines. (Ch. 10.2.2)

---

## B

**BC (storage-node contact, buried contact):** Contact landing on the AA beside the word line, connecting the cell transistor to the capacitor. Etched self-aligned to the nitride cap. (Ch. 1.6.3, 16.3)

**BCAT (buried-channel array transistor):** DRAM access transistor whose word line is buried in a trench across the AA, with a channel wrapping under it. (Ch. 1.3)

**BCl₃ step:** Waferless-autoclean step that removes titanium fluoride deposits by halogen exchange (TiF₄ → TiCl₄, B takes the F). (Ch. 9.3.1)

**Bottom reaction probability (β):** Probability that a radical reaching the metal at the slot bottom reacts. Sets k. (Ch. 3.2.2)

**Breakthrough (BT):** Short step that removes WOₓ and TiOₓNᵧ skins left by CMP. Reference NF₃/Ar, 5 s. (Ch. 4.7, 7.3.1)

**BTBT (band-to-band tunnelling):** GIDL component driven by high field at the gate–junction overlap. (Ch. 13.6.1)

---

## C

**Cap (nitride cap):** SiN filling the slot above the recessed conductor; isolates the word line from the contacts. (Ch. 1.4.4, 16.2)

**χ (chlorine fraction):** Q(Cl₂) / [Q(Cl₂) + Q(SF₆)] in the main step. Sets the TiN–W rate ratio. Reference 0.6. (Ch. 4.4)

**CMP plane:** Top of the hard mask after tungsten CMP, where the recess starts (z = −30 nm in the field). (Ch. 1.5, 2.4)

**Coburn–Winters model:** Γ_bottom/Γ_top = K/(K + β(1 − K)). (Ch. 3.2.2)

**Corner shadowing:** Reduced ion flux at the slot corners, where the TiN sits, as the recess deepens. (Ch. 3.5.2)

---

## D

**DC (bit-line contact):** Contact landing on the AA centre between two word lines. (Ch. 1.6.3)

**Dishing:** Metal surface below the surrounding mask after CMP. Array 3 nm, pads 8 nm. (Ch. 2.4.3)

**Δ_ov (overlap):** x_j − z_gate. Positive when the gate overlaps the junction. Reference +2 nm; window −3 to +7 nm. (Ch. 1.4.1)

**Δ_TW (TiN–W step):** TiN top minus W top, measured upward (positive = horn). Spec |Δ_TW| ≤ 3 nm. (Ch. 11)

**Dummy word line:** Non-functional word line at the array edge that absorbs patterning and CMP edge effects. (Ch. 10.2.2)

**DWF (dual-work-function) word line:** TiN/W lower part plus n⁺ polysilicon upper part, which lowers GIDL at the gate edge. Needs a second (polysilicon) recess. (Ch. 14.1)

---

## E

**Edge tilt:** Ion tilt at the wafer edge from edge-ring wear; makes horns asymmetric between the two walls of a slot. (Ch. 9.5.2)

**EPC (etch per cycle):** Material removed per ALE cycle. (Ch. 6.5)

**Erosion:** Thinning of the hard mask in dense arrays during CMP. Array 2 nm. (Ch. 2.4.3)

**Etchback-only flow:** Removing the tungsten overburden with plasma instead of CMP, then recessing. Has a field-clear endpoint. (Ch. 2.5)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving average used for chamber-constant feedback. λ = 0.3. (Ch. 15.5.3)

---

## F

**Feed-forward:** Adjusting the main-step time from measured incoming height and CD. 0.43 s per nm of height; −0.37 s per nm of CD. (Ch. 15.5.2)

**Field clear:** In etchback-only flows, the moment the overburden is removed from the field; a real endpoint. (Ch. 2.5, 15.1)

**First-wafer effect:** Recess difference of the first wafers after a clean or idle, from wall state. (Ch. 9.2.3)

**Φ (loading factor):** Parameter of ER(f) = ER(0)/(1 + Φf). Reference 1.9. (Ch. 3.7.3)

---

## G

**Gate edge:** The top of the conductor where it faces the gate oxide and the silicon; in a TiN/W word line, the TiN top. (Ch. 1.3.2, 11.2.1)

**GIDL (gate-induced drain leakage):** Leakage at the gate–junction overlap driven by band-to-band and trap-assisted tunnelling. (Ch. 1.4.1, 13.6)

---

## H

**h (hole depth):** Depth of the metal surface below the local mask top. Array: 3 nm at the start, 88 nm at the end. (Ch. 3.3)

**h_eff (effective conductor height):** Length-weighted conductor height over AA and isolation. Reference 108 nm. (Ch. 1.4.2)

**Horn:** TiN left standing above the tungsten along the slot wall. Removed laterally by the trim. (Ch. 11.2)

---

## I

**ICP (inductively coupled plasma):** Reactor in which a coil sets the plasma density and a separate chuck bias sets the ion energy. (Ch. 5)

**IED (ion energy distribution):** Bimodal at 13.56 MHz for light ions; F⁺ spans ≈ 38–83 eV at a 60 eV mean. (Ch. 6.2)

---

## K

**Keyhole void:** Void in the nitride cap along the wall, formed when a re-entrant TiN slit pinches off. Source of WL–BC shorts. (Ch. 11.3.3)

---

## L

**λ (seam penetration length):** δ/√s; depth to which fluorine etches an open seam ahead of the surface. (Ch. 12.2.1)

**Landing step:** Slower, lower-energy final main step for the last 10–15 nm. (Ch. 7.4)

**Loading:** Dependence of the recess rate on the exposed metal fraction f. (Ch. 3.7)

---

## N

**Nucleation layer:** 1–2 nm boron- or silicon-containing tungsten grown first on the TiN; high resistivity; etches faster than bulk W. (Ch. 2.3.1, 12.4.2)

---

## O

**OCD (optical critical dimension, scatterometry):** In-line measurement of the array conductor depth. ±0.7 nm vs. TEM. (Ch. 15.3)

---

## P

**Pad (SWD pad):** Wide (≈ 40 nm) word-line landing at the sub-word-line driver. Recesses ≈ 12 nm deeper than the array. (Ch. 3.4, 10.3)

**Passing word line:** Word line running through isolation beside an AA it does not gate; involved in row hammer. (Ch. 1.4.3)

**PT (post-treatment):** N₂/H₂ plasma after the trim that removes adsorbed halogens and nitrides the W top. (Ch. 4.8.2)

**Pullback:** TiN recessed below the tungsten top, leaving a slit. (Ch. 11.3)

**Punch-through (seam):** Deep opening of a voided seam into a slot by fluorine. (Ch. 12.2.3)

---

## Q

**Quasi-ALE:** Rapid alternation of modify and remove steps without full saturation; most of ALE's benefit at several times its rate. (Ch. 6.5.4)

---

## R

**R (recess depth):** Metal removed, measured from the field mask top: 90 nm reference (85 nm in the eroded array). (Ch. 1.5)

**r (TiN–W rate ratio):** ER_TiN/ER_W. Open-area 0.97; in-slot average 0.947 in the reference main step. (Ch. 4.4, 11.1)

**R_SWL:** Sub-word-line resistance. Reference 13.9 kΩ; +0.9% per nm of extra recess. (Ch. 1.4.2)

**Retention tail:** Small population of cells with high leakage that sets the refresh interval. (Ch. 1.1.2, 13.6)

**Row hammer:** Disturbance of a cell by repeated activation of a neighboring or passing word line. (Ch. 1.4.3, 10.4.3)

---

## S

**SAC (self-aligned contact):** Contact etch that uses the nitride cap as a stop when misaligned toward the word line. (Ch. 16.3)

**SADP / SAQP:** Self-aligned double / quadruple patterning of word lines; leaves alternating slot widths. (Ch. 10.2.1)

**Seam:** Low-density grain-boundary plane on the slot centreline where conformal W growth fronts meet. (Ch. 2.3.2, 12.1)

**Season:** Short plasma step after the waferless autoclean that sets the wall to a reproducible state. (Ch. 9.3)

**Shoulder:** 1.4 nm step where the slot narrows from 13.8 nm (mask) to 11 nm (silicon); the oxide ledge loses ≈ 1.3 nm. (Ch. 2.1.3, 13.2.2)

**Slit:** 2 nm gap between tungsten and oxide left by TiN pullback. (Ch. 11.3)

**Slot (conductor slot, w_s):** Space inside the gate oxide that holds TiN and W. 11.0 nm in silicon. (Ch. 2.1)

**Stub:** Unrecessed metal under a particle; becomes a WL–contact short. (Ch. 9.6.1)

**SWD (sub-word-line driver):** Driver at the edge of a sub-array; its word-line pads are recessed with the array. (Ch. 1.2.2)

**SWL (sub-word line):** Segment of word line driven by one SWD. Reference 52 µm, 1024 cells. (Ch. 1.2.2)

**Synchronized pulsing:** Source and bias switched together (1 kHz, 50%). Lowers k and charging. (Ch. 6.3)

---

## T

**TAT (trap-assisted tunnelling):** GIDL component through traps in the gap; raised by plasma damage. (Ch. 13.6.1)

**TiN veil:** TiN left on the wall above the silicon surface; the gate edge then reaches the surface. (Ch. 11.2.3)

**Trim (TR):** Low-energy Cl₂/Ar/O₂ step that removes horns laterally. Reference 6 s. (Ch. 7.3.3, 11.4)

**tWR (write recovery time):** Time to write a full level into the cell; limited by underlap resistance. (Ch. 10.4.2)

---

## U

**Underlap:** Negative overlap; the gate edge sits below the junction, adding series resistance. (Ch. 1.4.1)

---

## V

**V-notch:** V-shaped opening of the seam at the conductor top. Reference ≈ 5 nm deep, ≈ 1.2 nm wide. (Ch. 12.2.2)

**VRT (variable retention time):** Cells whose leakage switches between states; linked to metastable traps at the gate edge. (Ch. 13.6.4)

**VUV (vacuum ultraviolet):** Plasma photons above the SiO₂ band gap that create trapped charge; the slot shields the gate edge. (Ch. 13.4.1)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless autoclean):** Per-wafer chamber clean: NF₃/O₂ for W, BCl₃/Cl₂ for Ti, then season. (Ch. 9.3)

**w_s, w_t, w_W:** Conductor slot width (11.0 nm), WL trench width at the Si surface (18.0 nm), W core width (7.0 nm). (Ch. 1.3.3, 2.1)

**WF₆:** Tungsten hexafluoride, the volatile product of W etching in fluorine (bp 17 °C). (Ch. 4.1)

**Word line (WL):** Gate line of the cell transistors; buried in the 6F² BCAT. (Ch. 1)

---

## X

**x_j:** Storage-node junction depth on the trench wall. Reference 62 nm. (Ch. 1.4.1)

**XRF (X-ray fluorescence):** Measures the tungsten remaining per area, giving average conductor height independent of shape. (Ch. 15.3)

---

## Z

**z_r:** Depth of the conductor top below the silicon surface. Reference 60 nm, spec 55–65 nm. (Ch. 1.4.1, 10)

**z_m, z_p:** Metal-top and polysilicon-top depths in a DWF word line. 75 nm and 52 nm. (Ch. 14.1)
